NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Policy ID: FIN-REIM-005
Version: 1.8
Effective Date: 01 February 2026

1. Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by employees while performing their official duties.

2. Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for the following expenses:
Business travel.
Hotel accommodation.
Client meetings.
Approved meals during business travel.
Internet expenses for eligible remote employees.
Approved training and certification fees.
Local transportation for official business.
Personal purchases and expenses unrelated to business activities are not reimbursable.

3. Travel Reimbursement Limits
3.1 Domestic Travel
Employees are eligible for the following hotel limits per night:
Junior employees: INR 3,000.
Mid-level employees: INR 4,500.
Senior employees: INR 6,500.
Department heads: INR 9,000.

3.2 Local Transportation
Taxi expenses are reimbursable when public transportation is impractical.
Employees should use company-approved transportation providers wherever available.
Personal vehicle usage requires prior manager approval.
Fuel reimbursement for approved personal vehicle usage is INR 12 per kilometre.

4. Meal Allowance
Employees travelling for business may claim meal expenses up to:
Breakfast: INR 250.
Lunch: INR 450.
Dinner: INR 550.
Alcoholic beverages and personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.

5. Internet Reimbursement
Eligible remote employees can claim up to INR 1,000 per month for home internet expenses.
The following conditions apply:
The employee must be approved for remote work.
An internet service provider invoice is required.
Mobile data top-ups are not covered.
Unused monthly allowance cannot be carried forward.

6. Training and Certification
Employees can claim up to INR 20,000 annually for approved professional certifications.
Prior written manager and HR approval is mandatory.
Courses must be relevant to the employee's current role or an approved career development plan.
Employees must submit proof of course completion.

7. Claim Submission Process
Employees must:
Log in to the expense management portal.
Select the relevant expense category.
Enter the expense date and amount.
Upload original invoices or valid digital receipts.
Provide a business justification.
Submit the claim to their reporting manager.

8. Submission Deadlines
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
Claims submitted after 30 days require an exception approved by the Finance Manager.
Claims older than 90 days are generally rejected unless an exceptional waiver is granted.

9. Approval Process
Claims up to INR 5,000 require reporting manager approval.
Claims between INR 5,001 and INR 25,000 require manager and Finance approval.
Claims exceeding INR 25,000 require department head and Finance Controller approval.
All claims are subject to policy compliance and supporting documentation.

10. Reimbursement Timeline
Finance reviews complete claims within 5 working days.
Approved reimbursements are processed within 7 working days after approval.
Incomplete claims are returned to employees for correction.

11. Fraud and Misuse
Submitting fabricated invoices, duplicate claims, or personal expenses as business expenses is prohibited.
Violations may result in claim rejection and disciplinary action.
