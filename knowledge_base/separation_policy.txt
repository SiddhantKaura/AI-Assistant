NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
EMPLOYEE SEPARATION POLICY

Policy ID: HR-SEP-004
Version: 2.2
Effective Date: 01 January 2026

1. Purpose
This policy describes the procedures and responsibilities associated with employee separation from NovaTech Solutions.

2. Types of Separation
Employee separation may occur through:
Voluntary resignation.ß
Retirement.
Termination due to performance or misconduct.
Mutual separation agreement.
End of fixed-term employment contract.
Death of an employee.

3. Resignation Process
Employees must submit their resignation through the HR portal or official email.
The resignation date is considered the start of the notice period.
HR will acknowledge the resignation within 2 working days.
The reporting manager will initiate the knowledge transfer process.

4. Notice Period
The standard notice period is determined by employment category:
Employees on probation: 15 calendar days.
Confirmed employees with less than 3 years of service: 30 calendar days.
Confirmed employees with 3 or more years of service: 60 calendar days.
Senior management employees: 90 calendar days.
Notice period waivers or reductions require written approval from HR and the department head.

5. Notice Period Buyout
Employees may request a notice period buyout.
Buyout approval is subject to business requirements.
The buyout amount is calculated using the employee's applicable salary components and the unserved notice period.
The company may waive the notice period in exceptional circumstances.

6. Knowledge Transfer
Before the last working day, employees must:
Document ongoing projects.
Transfer ownership of assigned tasks.
Share relevant technical documentation.
Hand over client and stakeholder information.
Return company-issued assets.
The reporting manager must confirm completion of knowledge transfer.

7. Exit Clearance
Clearance is required from:
Reporting Manager.
Human Resources.
Information Technology.
Finance Department.
Administration Department.
All company assets must be returned before final settlement.

8. Full and Final Settlement
Full and Final Settlement may include:
Salary payable up to the last working day.
Eligible unused Earned Leave encashment.
Approved unpaid reimbursements.
Applicable bonuses, if eligible.
Notice period recovery, where applicable.
Outstanding employee advances or other recoveries.
The target processing period is 30 calendar days after the last working day and completion of required clearances, subject to applicable law and pending documentation.

9. Experience and Relieving Letters
Employees who complete the separation process are eligible for an experience letter.
Relieving letters are issued after clearance completion.
Employment verification requests must be directed to HR.
Pending disciplinary or legal matters may require additional review.

10. Confidentiality
Employees must continue to protect confidential company information after separation.
Employees must not retain company source code, customer data, internal documents, or intellectual property without written authorization.
