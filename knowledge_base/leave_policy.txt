LEAVE MANAGEMENT POLICY

Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Last Updated: 15 September 2026

1. Purpose
This policy defines the leave entitlements, eligibility criteria, approval processes, and rules for employees of NovaTech Solutions.

2. Types of Leave
2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Every permanent employee receives 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year.
Casual Leave can be taken for personal reasons or emergencies.
Employees can take a maximum of 3 consecutive Casual Leave days.
Unused Casual Leave expires at the end of the calendar year.
Casual Leave cannot be encashed.

2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
Employees receive 10 days of Sick Leave annually.
A medical certificate is required when Sick Leave exceeds 2 consecutive working days.
Sick Leave cannot be transferred to another employee.
Unused Sick Leave cannot be encashed.
Sick Leave can be taken during emergencies without prior approval, but the employee must notify their manager as soon as possible.

2.3 Earned Leave (EL)
Permanent employees receive 18 days of Earned Leave annually.
Earned Leave accrues at 1.5 days per month.
Employees can carry forward up to 30 unused Earned Leave days.
Earned Leave exceeding 30 days will expire at the end of the financial year.
Earned Leave requests should be submitted at least 7 calendar days in advance.
Earned Leave can be encashed during separation according to company rules.

2.4 Maternity Leave
Eligible employees receive maternity benefits according to applicable Indian law.
Employees should inform HR as early as reasonably possible.
Relevant medical documentation may be requested.

2.5 Paternity Leave
Eligible male employees receive 10 working days of paid Paternity Leave.
Paternity Leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
Paternity Leave cannot be carried forward.

3. Leave Approval Process
Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal.
Casual Leave should preferably be requested 2 working days in advance.
Earned Leave requires 7 calendar days' advance notice.
The reporting manager is responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.
HR must approve leave requests exceeding 15 consecutive calendar days.

4. Leave During Notice Period
Employees serving notice must obtain manager and HR approval before taking leave.
Unapproved leave may be treated as unpaid leave.
Earned Leave settlement will be handled during final settlement.
Leave cannot automatically extend the notice period.

5. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
Employees who exhaust their paid leave balance may request Leave Without Pay.
LWP requires approval from the reporting manager and HR.
Salary deductions are calculated based on the number of unpaid working days.

6. Important Rules
Employees must not submit overlapping leave requests.
False medical documentation may result in disciplinary action.
Employees must ensure proper work handover before planned extended leave.
