NOVATECH SOLUTIONS
HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY

Policy ID: HR-GEN-002
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees receive a 60-minute lunch break.
Employees are expected to complete 8 working hours daily.
Flexible working hours are available with manager approval.
Employees working remotely must remain available during core business hours of 11 AM to 4 PM.

2. Attendance Policy
Employees must record attendance through the company attendance portal.
Employees are allowed a maximum of 3 late arrivals per month without disciplinary action.
Arrivals exceeding 15 minutes after the scheduled start time are considered late.
Employees must notify their manager if they cannot attend work.
Repeated unexplained absences may lead to disciplinary action.

3. Work From Home Policy
Employees may request up to 8 Work From Home days per month.
Work From Home requires prior manager approval.
Employees must have a stable internet connection and a suitable workspace.
Employees must attend scheduled meetings while working remotely.
Certain roles requiring physical presence may not be eligible for Work From Home.

4. Probation Policy
New employees undergo a probation period of 6 months.
Performance is reviewed at the end of the third and sixth months.
The probation period may be extended by up to 3 months with written approval from HR.
Confirmation of employment is communicated through an official email.

5. Employee Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice annually.
Review periods are January-June and July-December.
Managers evaluate employees based on performance, teamwork, ownership, and delivery.
Employees may submit self-assessments before their review meetings.
Salary revisions are subject to performance, budget availability, and management approval.

6. Code of Conduct
Employees must:
Treat colleagues respectfully.
Maintain confidentiality of company information.
Avoid discrimination and harassment.
Follow information security policies.
Disclose potential conflicts of interest.
Use company resources responsibly.
7. Anti-Harassment Policy
NovaTech Solutions maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward workplace harassment.
Employees may report concerns to HR or the Internal Committee.
All complaints will be handled confidentially and fairly.
Retaliation against individuals reporting misconduct is prohibited.
Complaints involving sexual harassment will be handled under applicable Indian law.

8. Employee Benefits
Eligible permanent employees receive:
Medical insurance coverage of INR 5,00,000 annually.
Annual wellness allowance of INR 8,000.
Access to learning and development programs.
Employee assistance and counselling services.

9. Asset Management
Employees are responsible for company-issued laptops, accessories, and identification cards.
Lost or damaged assets must be reported to IT immediately.
Company assets must be returned during separation.
